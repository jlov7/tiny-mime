## 0.2.0

Tiny Mime is a local geometric-motion prototype with four workspaces: Private
Play, Rehearsal, Creator Capture and synthetic Study operations.

- Deterministic motion playback, strict score validation and continuous
  noncontact checks for a fixed cast of two or three shapes.
- Eleven authored first-act drafts, five motion references and three prepared
  directing comparisons. The fixture round repeats its first take.
- Two-guess rounds with explicit reveal, replay, expiry and interrupted-request
  recovery. The live Responses API adapter is disabled by default.
- Rehearsal imports, numeric edit forks with undo, saved candidate versions,
  timestamped notes, interval loops and synchronized comparisons.
- Offline creator replays and score-only candidate inspection packets.
- Protected synthetic study rehearsals, masking, retention, rating imports and
  analysis. Human study activation and real-data analysis remain separate work.
- A standalone authored preview that opens without installation, plus a
  content-addressed source/preview bundle and fresh-install release rehearsal.
- Offline provider readiness and support diagnostics. The runtime timing
  diagnostic defaults to a plan and requires explicit approved execution.

Human comprehension, enjoyment, comparative benefit, physical-device behavior
and live interactive API qualification are not established by this release.
